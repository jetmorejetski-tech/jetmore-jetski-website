What We Fix On-Site

We handle a wide range of repairs right at your location:

  • Electrical and computer diagnostics — fault codes pulled with manufacturer-grade scan tools

Common Calls We Get From Massapequa

A few of the situations we see most often in this area:

"It ran fine last fall, now it won't start." Classic post-winter fuel system issue — stale gas, gummed injectors, or a dead battery. Usually resolved in one visit.

"It's throwing a code and the dealer wants three weeks." Dealer backlogs in summer are brutal. We can often get to you within a day or two and pull codes right on-site.

"I bought it used and something feels off." We do pre-purchase inspections too — but if you already bought and something's wrong, a full diagnostic will pinpoint it fast.

"It sucked up something and now it's vibrating bad." Impeller damage. We carry wear rings and common impeller sizes for Sea-Doo and Yamaha. Often same-day.

Seasonal Services in Massapequa

Repair is just part of what we do. We also take care of the start and end of your riding season — services that are easy to put off and expensive to skip.

Winterizing

Proper winterization protects your engine, cooling system, and fuel system from freeze damage over the off-season. We come to your location in the fall, run the full winterization procedure, fog the engine, stabilize the fuel, and flush the cooling system. Everything your machine needs to wake up healthy when spring hits.

Shrink Wrap

After winterizing, we can shrink wrap your jet ski right on your trailer or lift. A properly fitted wrap keeps moisture, UV, and rodents out all winter. Marinas charge a premium for this — we come to your driveway and do it for less hassle.

Spring Commissioning

We also handle spring startups: de-winterize, check all systems, address anything that developed over winter, and send you into the season with confidence. Start spring right instead of discovering a problem on your first ride out.

Insurance Claims — We Handle It End to End
