What We Fix On-Site

We handle a wide range of repairs right at your dock, driveway, or storage yard:

  • Engine and compression issues — rough idle, no-start, overheating
  • Impeller and wear ring replacement — common after sand, gravel, or debris ingestion
  • Electrical and computer diagnostics — fault codes pulled with manufacturer-grade scan tools
  • Water ingestion — one of the most urgent situations a PWC owner can face; we treat it as a priority
  • Fuel system service — carb cleaning, injector problems, fuel pump failures
  • Cooling system flushes — essential maintenance that prevents expensive engine damage
  • Starter, battery, and charging system troubleshooting

Seasonal Services in Bellmore

Repair is just part of what we do. We also handle the start and end of your riding season — services that are easy to put off and expensive to skip.

Winterizing

Proper winterization protects your engine, cooling system, and fuel system from freeze damage over the off-season. We come to your location in the fall, fog the engine, stabilize the fuel, and flush the cooling system — everything your machine needs to wake up healthy in spring. Want the full rundown? Read our jet ski winterizing guide.

Shrink Wrap & Storage

After winterizing, we can shrink wrap your jet ski right on your trailer or lift — or store it for you at our Seaford facility over the winter. A properly fitted wrap keeps moisture, UV, and rodents out all season.

Spring Commissioning

We also handle spring startups: de-winterize, check all systems, address anything that developed over winter, and send you into the season with confidence.
